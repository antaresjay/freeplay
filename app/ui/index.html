Freeplay

A game trainer for Windows that is free, open source, and does not want your email address.

I play single player games and I use trainers. Infinite money in a Witcher game, skipping a mission timer I have already failed six times. Nobody else is affected. It is my save file and my evening.

Every way of doing that was bad in a different way. The good one wants a subscription. The free ones are executables from sites buried in ads that tell you to turn off your antivirus. Cheat Engine can do anything, which is also the problem.

A program that attaches to your games and writes into their memory is a program you should be able to read before you trust it. That is why the source is open, and it is the whole point rather than a marketing line.

Single player only

Freeplay refuses to attach to anything running an anti-cheat, and the check runs before the process handle is used for anything. EasyAntiCheat, BattlEye, Vanguard, GameGuard, XIGNCODE, PunkBuster and Denuvo Anti-Cheat are all on the list. Cheating in multiplayer ruins the game for people who did nothing to deserve it, and it gets accounts banned.
