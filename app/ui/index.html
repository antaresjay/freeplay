            Overlay while you play
            A panel over the game for switching cheats on without alt tabbing. Windowed and borderless only, since exclusive fullscreen draws over everything.
          
    
          
        
    

        
    
          
    
            Shortcut
            Click the box, then press the keys you want.
            
    
              
              
            
    
          
    
          
    
            Freeplay watches the keyboard for it
            Many games swallow every key while they have focus, so an ordinary shortcut never arrives. Freeplay compares each key against the combination on the left and nothing else. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent, and it stops the moment you switch this off.
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            Leave the game's focus alone
            Some games pause the moment another window takes focus. With this on the panel never takes it: the game keeps running behind it, but the panel is click only, so numbers cannot be typed into it.

    Freeplay
    
            
          
    
          
    A game trainer for Windows. Free, open source, no account needed.
    
          
    It finds your installed games, attaches to one and turns on cheats. Tables other people have published arrive on their own. If nobody has written one for your game, import a Cheat Engine .CT file or search the game's memory and make one.
    
          
    No email address, no password, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and nothing about you or your machine. Claiming a name is optional and is a key rather than an account. Every request Freeplay can make is listed here, and the two switches in Settings turn all of them off.
    
          
    It will not attach to anything running an anti-cheat, so it is single player only.
    
          
    
            
            
            
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Every request it can make
    
          
    
            
    The table listOnce at startup. Nothing goes with it.
    
            
    What people have shared for a gameWhen you open that game's page. Sends the game's file name and version.
    
            
    A table you asked forWhen you press Use table.
    
            
    A table you are sharingWhen you press Share yours.
    
            
    Your voteWhen you answer whether a table worked.
    
            
    A name you are claimingWhen you claim one.
